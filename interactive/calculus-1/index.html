Calculus 1

Twenty-nine interactive lessons on limits, derivatives and integrals. Each one starts with a question, gives you a graph to work with, and asks you to commit to an answer before it shows you one.

The lessons are numbered in the order most courses teach them, from the first idea of a limit to the average value of a function. If you are reviewing for a test, go straight to the topic you need. Teaching with them? Start with the instructor guide.

How to use these lessons

Try each question before you open its answer. The step-by-step solvers count how many steps you get right on the first try, so a wrong guess costs you a point but nothing else. What you work out yourself is what you will remember on the test.
