As x grows

Divide by the highest power

Every term of the form \tfrac{c}{x^p} with p>0 approaches 0 as x\to\pm\infty. So divide the numerator and the denominator by the highest power of x in the denominator, and most terms disappear:

\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x^2+2x-1}{x^2+5}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3+\frac{2}{x}-\frac{1}{x^2}}{1+\frac{5}{x^2}}=\frac{3+0-0}{1+0}=3.

The line y=3 is a horizontal asymptote. Doing this for any rational function gives three cases, decided by the degrees of the top and bottom.

Why should only the leading terms matter? At x=1000, the term 3x^2 is three million, while 2x-1 is under two thousand.

Roots need care. Choose \tfrac{\sqrt{4x^2+1}}{x+1} in the graph and compare f(x) with f(-x). Because \sqrt{x^2}=|x|, the limits at \infty and -\infty are different.

    Common mistakes

    Writing ∞/∞ = 1

    Like \tfrac00, the form \tfrac{\infty}{\infty} is indeterminate. It tells you to simplify, not what the answer is.

    Forgetting the sign at −∞

    An odd power of a negative number is negative. When the top's degree is higher, check the sign of the leading terms for x\to-\infty separately.

    Treating √(x²) as x

    For negative x, \sqrt{x^2}=-x. This is where different limits at the two ends usually come from.
