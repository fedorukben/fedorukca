The approach

The question comes first.

1

Start with a problem

Each idea arrives as a problem to work on, not a paragraph to read. Definitions and examples get you started; the discovery is yours.

2

You do the proving

The results are left for you to establish. A careful sequence of exercises makes each one reachable, and each one makes the next possible.

3

Hard things are called hard

Nothing is hand-waved and nothing pretends to be easy. When a step takes real effort, you are told so, because the effort is the point.

About Fedoruk

Learning you earn, not receive.

Fedoruk is an educational brand built on inquiry-based learning. It publishes books for university students and self-learners, and interactive lessons, all in mathematics and computing.

Everything is written by Benjamin D. Fedoruk, who holds a BSc in Applied and Industrial Mathematics and an MEd and writes from Ontario, Canada.
